## About Me

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, in the Armsworth Lab, funded by the National Science Foundation and working with NatureServe, the U.S. Geological Survey, and the National Wildlife Federation.

My work sits on one recurring problem. A policy sets a target. A metric gets chosen because it is easy to count. Policy then optimizes the metric instead of the outcome. My dissertation shows this in protected areas, where acreage keeps rising while protection of the ecosystems that need it does not. The same gap runs through clean energy siting, EV charging deployment, and corporate sustainability disclosure.

I build the analysis that finds the gap, and I write the memo that gets someone to act on it.
